# Research / Publications

A collection of my research papers and technical reports, spanning renewable energy, carbon capture and storage, and environmental economics.

## Papers

**[The Potential of Carbon Capture and Storage: A Technology and Risk Assessment](./ccs-potential.md)**
Reviews CO₂ capture technologies (post-combustion, pre-combustion, oxy-fuel), storage options, and the environmental, socio-economic, and regulatory barriers to large-scale CCS deployment. Concludes with policy recommendations to make CCS a more viable climate mitigation option.

**[Ecosystem Services Valuation: A Case Study of Offshore Wind Farm Development](./ecosystem-services-valuation.md)**
Traces the development of Ecosystem Services Valuation (ESV) as a concept and policy tool, reviews key valuation methods (Travel Cost, Hedonic Pricing, Contingent Valuation), and applies this framework to a hypothetical offshore wind farm to assess trade-offs between renewable energy deployment and ecosystem value.

**[Off-Grid PV Systems for Rural Electrification: A Resource Discussion](./off-grid-pv-maiduguri.md)**
Examines the potential for off-grid solar PV to electrify rural Maiduguri, Nigeria, using solar irradiation data, appliance-level energy demand modeling, and a cost-benefit analysis, including payback period calculations for a proposed 150kWp system.

**[Evaluation of Off-Grid PV Systems as a Supplement for Grid Extension for Rural Electrification](./off-grid-pv-project-proposal.md)**
A fuller technical proposal building on the above, covering solar PV technology and system design, component sizing methodology (batteries, inverters, charge controllers), carbon emission savings calculations, and a project implementation timeline.
